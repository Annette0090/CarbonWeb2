Our Story

About Carbon AV

Ghana's Creative Force in Events & Communications

Carbon AV Ltd is a full-service agency and consultancy headquartered in Accra, Ghana. We operate at the intersection of creativity, strategy, and technology — delivering events, audio visual productions, and communications campaigns that captivate audiences and drive results.

From the prestige of the EMY Africa Awards to high-energy sporting spectacles like PruRide Ghana, we've built a portfolio that defines the gold standard of event production in West Africa.

At Carbon AV, our team of experienced professionals rely on a consultative approach to turn your vision into an unforgettable experience. Using our strengths in event management, design, and styling, we take your dreams and turn them into well-executed, custom events.
